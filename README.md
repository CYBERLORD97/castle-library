# The Castle Library
A free library of books. Read them online, or download them as EPUB files to read on any device. There are no subscriptions and no accounts.

# What you can do

Browse the shelves by subject, or search by title, author, or language.
Read a book on the lectern. Turn the pages, and the page-turn sound plays as you go. You can switch the sound and background music on or off.
Light a candle to change the mood of the room.
Highlight passages as you read. Your highlights are saved in your browser and listed in the Highlights panel.
Download any available book as an EPUB and keep it.
Support the shelves through the link in the footer, if you'd like to help keep the library free.
Your reading progress, study shelf, and highlights are saved in your own browser. They are not stored on our servers and do not follow you to another device.
Some books are prepared the first time they are opened, so the first load may take a few seconds.
